Growth Design Published
01.

COTIT

Role: Growth Designer

View Case Study

The Problem

The legacy monolithic platform suffered from 4+ second load times and a fragmented checkout process, causing a 65% mobile drop-off rate.

The Strategy

Decouple the frontend layer to achieve sub-second load times while redesigning the checkout flow to require zero page reloads.

The Solution

A custom Next.js frontend integrated natively with Shopify Plus APIs, featuring a hyper-optimized, glassmorphic UI architecture.

The Outcome

Increased mobile conversion by 42%, reduced average load time to 0.8s, and generated a 2.5x ROI within the first quarter post-launch.

Web Development Published
02.

KAWAN PASANG

Role: Web Developer

View Case Study

The Problem

Patients were navigating 4 different portals to view lab results, message doctors, and pay bills, leading to clinic call-center overload.

The Strategy

Unify all functions into a single, high-contrast, accessible application focusing strictly on reducing time-to-completion for core tasks.

The Solution

A pristine, component-driven React dashboard enforcing WCAG AA compliance and prioritizing legible typography over dense data views.

The Outcome

Decreased clinic call volume by 31%, increased digital bill payment by 60%, and drastically improved patient satisfaction scores.

UI/UX Design Published
03.

BAZARTANI

Role: UI/UX Designer

View Case Study

The Problem

The startup had powerful underlying technology but looked indistinguishable from a dozen other early-stage competitors.

The Strategy

Position the brand purely through the lens of enterprise security and institutional reliability, avoiding playful startup tropes.

The Solution

A severe, dark-mode architectural identity paired with a high-performance marketing site emphasizing micro-interactions and data visualization.

The Outcome

Secured 14 enterprise beta partners within the first month of launch, directly aiding in the closure of a $12M Series A round.
