Who is bangjeje.dev?

bangjeje.dev is an independent digital design and development studio founded by Jajang Nurjaman.

The studio helps startups, SMEs, and growing businesses transform ideas into meaningful digital products through product design, website development, and business-focused digital experiences.

Rather than simply creating beautiful interfaces, bangjeje.dev focuses on solving business problems, improving customer experiences, and delivering measurable digital value.
